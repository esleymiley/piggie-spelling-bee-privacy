Piggie Spelling Bee privacy policy

Piggits Games · Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Piggie Spelling Bee app for Android (package com.piggitsgames.spelling)

The short version

  • Piggie Spelling Bee has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no analytics.
  • A child's progress, settings and word lists are stored on the device and nowhere else.
  • The only information that leaves the device is what Google Play and our billing provider need to process a Piggie Plus purchase: a purchase receipt, a random identifier and basic app and device details. No name, email, address or location is involved.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app does not ask for any.

Information stored on the device

The app keeps its records in the app's own storage on the device:

  • learning progress, scores, mastered and missed words, starred words and save points;
  • settings such as the spelling level, the spelling policy (American or British) and parent reward settings;
  • word lists that a parent or child types in or imports, and an optional profile name typed in Settings;
  • the current state of a Piggie Plus subscription, so the app knows what to unlock while offline.

These records never leave the device unless you export them yourself (see Files you save). Your device's own Android backup may also copy the app's data to your Google Drive, under your Google account's backup settings. Uninstalling the app removes them from the device.

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, store or transmit names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, audio, precise or approximate location, advertising identifiers or browsing history. It has no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no social features. The app loads no content from the internet while it runs; every lesson, list and voice prompt is included in the app.

Purchases: Google Play and RevenueCat

Piggie Plus is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy; we never see payment details.

To check whether a device is entitled to Piggie Plus, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. When a purchase is made or checked, the app sends RevenueCat:

DataWhy
the purchase receipt (token) and product identifier from Google Playto confirm the purchase and its renewal or expiry
a random identifier that the app generates on the device (it is not linked to a Google account, name or email)to associate purchases with the device so that Plus can be restored
the app version, platform and operating-system version, the device's brand, model and locale, and the Google Play storefront countryso the service can answer with the right products and prices

RevenueCat keeps this purchase history on our behalf. It is encrypted in transit and is used only to run the subscription and to show us aggregate sales figures. It is not sold and not used for advertising. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy. To have a device's purchase record deleted, contact us (below); deleting it does not cancel a subscription, which is managed in Google Play.

Every purchase sits behind a grown-up check, and Google Play applies the purchase approval settings of the Google account on the device.

Children's privacy

Piggie Spelling Bee is made for children in Grade 1 and Grade 2 and is used with the help of a parent or teacher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app asks for no personal details (an optional profile name typed in Settings stays on the device and in backups you save), has no account, no chat and no sharing. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in some other way, contact us and we will delete it.

Speech

Words are read aloud by the text-to-speech engine installed on the device (on most Android devices, Google's). The app sends the words to that engine on the device; nothing is recorded, and the app has no microphone permission. If your text-to-speech engine downloads voices, that happens under the engine's own policy.

Files you save

Progress backups and word-list exports are created only when you ask for them and are saved to the location you choose in the system "Save as" sheet. Importing a word list reads only the file you pick. These files are yours; the app keeps no copy.

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which it uses only for Google Play purchases and the entitlement check described above. It requests no other permission.

Security

On-device records are protected by Android's app sandbox. Purchase data is sent over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to handle information differently, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top.
